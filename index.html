Isogeny-based Cryptography Wiki

A collaborative, accessible reference for isogeny-based cryptographic schemes.

Welcome to the Isogeny-based Cryptography Wiki. This site serves as an open-source, mathematically rigorous, and accessible repository of knowledge detailing post-quantum cryptographic schemes built on the hardness of finding isogenies between elliptic curves.

1 Getting Started

If you are not familiar with cryptographic schemes, we recommend Mike Rosulek’s book. For the underlying mathematics, see Steven Galbraith’s book. You may also benefit from reading PhD theses in this area, for instance this list curated by Giacomo Pope.

You may also find good reference material in past schools, such as Bristol 2020, ANTS 2020, PCMI 2022, IPAM 2022, VaNTAGe 2022, CIMPA 2024, SLMath 2024, PQCSA 2025, and AWS 2026.

To engage with the community, consider joining activities such as the Isogeny Club, Leuven Isogeny Days, BIRS workshops, and Swissogeny Day.

3 Contributing

This wiki is maintained by the isogeny-crypto organization. If you spot a mathematical error, want to expand on a protocol’s performance metrics, or wish to add a new primitive:

  1. Fork the repository on GitHub.
  2. Create your article using a .qmd file in the schemes/ directory.
  3. Submit a Pull Request.
